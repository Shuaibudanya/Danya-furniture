About Us

Crafting Comfort, Building Trust

Danya Furniture was founded by Shuaibu Abdullahi Danya with one goal in mind: to bring beautiful, durable, and affordable furniture into every Nigerian home and office.

Based in Kano, we specialize in producing and supplying quality sofas, beds, chairs, dining sets, wardrobes, TV stands, and office furniture. Every piece we make combines strong craftsmanship with modern design, ensuring that our customers get real value for their money.

Whether you are furnishing a new home, upgrading your office, or looking for a custom furniture design, Danya Furniture is here to serve you with honesty, quality, and passion.
